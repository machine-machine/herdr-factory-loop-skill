# Packaging

- **Title:** one concrete promise, no colon stacking, ≤ 60 chars
- **Thumbnail:** the hook's first frame, one subject, one face, big readable contrast —
  text ≤ 1 cm on a phone
- **Description:** the promise in line 1, then chapters with timestamps, then the source list
- **Chapters:** one per beat, `mm:ss` — check every timestamp against the locked cut
- **Tags:** subject, place, era, one curiosity hook
- **End card:** where the next film is, one line
